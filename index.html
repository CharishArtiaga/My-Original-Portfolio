About

I'm Charish T. Artiaga, a 22-year-old Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at the College of Our Lady of Mercy of Pulilan Foundation, Inc.

My main skills are UI/UX design and graphic design. I enjoy turning ideas into interfaces and visuals that are simple to use and pleasant to look at, whether that means planning how a user moves through an app or building the graphics that give a brand its look.

I'm also currently learning QA testing. It's helping me think more carefully about how people use a product, spot problems before they reach users, and make sure what I design actually works as intended.

I'm always looking to learn more, improve my craft, and take on projects where design and quality come together.
